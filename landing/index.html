Features

Everything your game needs

📋
Game feed

See all upcoming games at a glance — capacity, location, date, and who's already in.

⏳
Smart waitlist

Game full? Join the waitlist. When someone drops out, you're automatically promoted — no chasing the group chat.

🔔
Push notifications

Get notified when you're added to a game, confirmed off the waitlist, or a game is cancelled.

👥
Team generator

Balanced random team splits generated in one tap. Teams are saved so everyone can check on match day.

🌐
Organisers on the web

Organiser accounts are created on the website — players just download the app and go. Clean and simple.

⚡
Real-time updates

Player lists update live as people join or leave. Everyone always sees the same thing.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

1
Organisers sign up on the web

Create your free organiser account at pitchapp.net/admin, then sign in to the app with the same details — the organiser tools appear automatically.

2
Create a game, players join in the app

Pick the sport, date, location and player cap. Players just download the app, sign up in seconds, and join with one tap — or the waitlist if it's full.

3
Pitch handles the rest

Waitlist promotions and notifications happen automatically. Generate balanced teams on match day. No spreadsheets, no group-chat chaos.
