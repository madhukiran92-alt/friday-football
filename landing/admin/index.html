FOR ORGANISERS

Run your games with Pitch

Pitch has two kinds of accounts: organisers and players. Organiser accounts are created right here — players simply download the app and sign up there.

1
Create your organiser account here

Fill in the form — name, email, password. That's it. This account comes with full organiser tools built in.

2
Sign in to the Pitch app

Download Pitch on iOS or Android and sign in with the same email and password. The app recognises you as an organiser automatically — you'll see the Admin tab next to your games.

3
Create games — your players join in the app

Set the sport, date, location and player cap. Players sign up in the app with one tap; Pitch runs the waitlist, notifications, and team splits for you.

🎉 Organiser accounts are free while Pitch is in early access.
